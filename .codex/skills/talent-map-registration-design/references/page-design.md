# Page Design

Desktop auth layout:

- Use a two-column grid: a fixed-width left auth module and a flexible right product module.
- Left module width should stay close to 420 px on desktop.
- Put a faint gray divider on the right edge of the left module, with low opacity and no heavy shadow.
- The right side presents the product promise, process steps, radar-style intelligence visual, and compact metrics.
- Keep the palette restrained: warm off-white surface, muted ink, amber accents, and soft green-blue data shapes.
- Avoid marketing-only hero treatment. The form remains the primary usable surface.

Mobile layout:

- Collapse to one column.
- Hide the decorative/product art if it competes with registration.
- Remove the vertical divider.
- Make the captcha image, input, and refresh control stack cleanly without horizontal overflow.

Form details:

- Keep auth tabs visible above the form.
- Keep labels concise.
- The email field and send-code button share a row on desktop and stack on mobile.
- The primary submit button spans the form width.
- Fine print should remain muted and short.
